## Personal And Cinematic Covers

Use only likeness references the current user has supplied and authorized. Match their reference consistently without copying a different person's tattoos, signature or biography. For a new brush title, create exact legible lettering for the actual words; a prior wordmark is not a reusable font. Keep brush lettering off the reading body.

Use one concrete topic-related scene, one hook, and one visual focus. A generated scene is an illustration, never proof of a business outcome, endorsement or event. Keep important words and the subject inside the tested safe crop. Do not add fake social metrics, legal documents, screenshots or attributed quotes.

Daniel 风暴实证风's body mapping uses short underlined keywords, a few strong judgments and a clear workflow quotation. Full design may use red/black accents; native X keeps semantic underlines, bold and unfilled quotes. No private character-card image or reference artwork is distributed; the cinematic poster preset includes Daniel’s user-confirmed textual appearance rules.

## Cinematic Poster Invocation

Honor a named style. When `daniel_cinematic_poster` is named, read its linked preset and execute its production prompt workflow. With no named style, it is a candidate for the listed content when a protagonist and strong poster hook fit; do not replace existing styles automatically.
