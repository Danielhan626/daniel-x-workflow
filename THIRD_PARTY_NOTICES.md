# Sources And Third-Party Boundaries

- The editorial theme catalog was developed while using [gzh-design-skill](https://github.com/isjiamu/gzh-design-skill). This repository describes style directions; it does not bundle that project's HTML component libraries, fonts or images. Install and consult the upstream project separately when exact WeChat components are needed. Its own license applies to its code.
- Publishing workflows may use separately installed tools such as [baoyu-skills](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-skills) or [x-article-publisher-skill](https://github.com/LearnPrompt/x-article-publisher-skill). None are bundled and no account access is inherited.
- Runtime packages `marked`, `sanitize-html` and `linkedom` retain their respective licenses, distributed with those packages. Exact dependency versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`.
- No character cards, likeness references, movie-poster references, generated media, commercial fonts or private example articles are distributed.

Names of platforms and upstream projects identify compatibility or inspiration and do not imply endorsement.
